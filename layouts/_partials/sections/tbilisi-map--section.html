04 Known for

Mtskheta-Mtianeti

Eastern Georgia · Greater Caucasus Mtskheta · Regional capital

From Georgia's ancient capital to dramatic mountain valleys, medieval fortresses and alpine landscapes.

MTSKHETA-MTIANETI / TRAVEL ESSENTIALS

Before you go

Scroll to explore
01 Getting there
By car

Around 30 minutes from Tbilisi to Mtskheta and 3–4 hours to Stepantsminda, depending on road conditions.

Minibus

Frequent services from Tbilisi's Didube station to Mtskheta, with departures to Gudauri and Stepantsminda.

02 Places to explore

Mtskheta

Ancient capital with UNESCO-listed churches and monasteries.

Stepantsminda

Mountain town beneath Mount Kazbek and Gergeti Trinity Church.

Gudauri

Mountain resort known for skiing, paragliding and spectacular views.

03 Things to do
  • Visit ancient churches and monasteries
  • Hike through the Greater Caucasus
  • Ski and snowboard in Gudauri
  • Explore mountain valleys and fortresses
04 Known for
  • Georgia's ancient religious heritage
  • Mount Kazbek and Gergeti Trinity Church
  • Georgian Military Highway
  • Alpine landscapes and winter sports

Scroll to explore

Around 8–10 hours from Tbilisi to Mestia. Zugdidi is approximately 4–5 hours away by car.

03 Things to do
  • Hike through Svaneti's alpine valleys
  • Explore medieval Svan tower villages
  • Take a boat trip through Martvili Canyon
  • Discover Megrelian cuisine and traditions
04 Known for
  • Medieval Svan defensive towers
  • UNESCO-listed Upper Svaneti
  • Mount Shkhara and Caucasus peaks
  • Megrelian cuisine and subtropical landscapes

SAMTSKHE-JAVAKHETI / TRAVEL ESSENTIALS

Before you go

Scroll to explore
01 Getting there
By car

Around 3–4 hours from Tbilisi to Akhaltsikhe. Vardzia and remote highland destinations require additional travel.

Minibus

Vardzia

Remarkable medieval cave monastery carved into a mountainside.

Akhaltsikhe

Historic town dominated by the restored Rabati Fortress.

Paravani Lake

Vast high-altitude lake surrounded by open volcanic landscapes.

03 Things to do
  • Explore Vardzia's ancient cave complex
  • Visit Rabati Fortress in Akhaltsikhe
  • Discover volcanic lakes and highland scenery
  • Explore medieval monasteries and castles
04 Known for
  • Vardzia cave monastery
  • Medieval fortresses and historic towns
  • Volcanic plateaus and alpine lakes
  • Multicultural heritage and traditions

Before you go

Scroll to explore

Around 1–1.5 hours from Tbilisi to Gori. Uplistsikhe is a short drive beyond the city.

03 Things to do
  • Explore the ancient caves of Uplistsikhe
  • Visit Gori Fortress and historic museums
  • Discover medieval churches and monasteries
  • Explore the Mtkvari Valley countryside
04 Known for
  • Uplistsikhe's ancient cave architecture
  • Medieval Georgian religious heritage
  • Gori and its complex Soviet history
  • Fertile valleys and agricultural traditions

Tbilisi

Eastern Georgia · Mtkvari Valley Capital of Georgia

Georgia's vibrant capital, where historic neighbourhoods, diverse architecture, food and contemporary culture meet.

TBILISI / TRAVEL ESSENTIALS

Before you go

Scroll to explore
01 Getting around
Metro & bus

Two metro lines and an extensive bus network connect central neighbourhoods and outer districts.

Taxi & walking

App-based taxis are widely used. The historic centre is walkable, although many streets are steep.

02 Places to explore

Old Tbilisi

Historic streets, colourful balconies and traditional bathhouses.

Sololaki

Elegant old buildings, ornate entrances and hidden courtyards.

Mtatsminda

Hillside neighbourhood with panoramic city views.

03 Things to do
  • Explore historic streets and courtyards
  • Experience traditional sulfur baths
  • Discover Georgian food and natural wine
  • Visit museums, galleries and local markets
04 Known for
  • Historic balconies and eclectic architecture
  • Traditional sulfur bathhouses
  • Georgian cuisine and wine culture
  • Creative neighbourhoods and contemporary culture
